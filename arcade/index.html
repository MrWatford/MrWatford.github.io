ARCADE

Arcade

Free-time games for when your work is done.

🕹️ Arcade rules

  1. Finish your classwork first.
  2. Mr. Watford decides when it's free time.
  3. When it's time to stop, save and close the game right away.
  4. Be a good sport, win or lose.

Looking for typing practice games? They're in the Typing Classroom.

⛏️ Blockhaven

Build, explore, and survive in a blocky world full of caves, villages, dungeons, and bosses. Play alone or together with classmates.

Keyboard and mouse. Worlds save on your device.

Play Blockhaven

🔷 Geometry Race

Jump, fly, and flip to the beat with one button. Twelve levels from Beginner to Expert, a boss finale, practice checkpoints, and custom cubes.

One button: works on any device.

Play Geometry Race

🛰️ Ringfall: Infinite Frontier

Explore a ring-shaped science frontier in first person. Grapple up cliffs, drive rovers, rescue research teams, and shut down a malfunctioning machine network. Twelve story missions.

Needs a keyboard and mouse (laptop, Chromebook, or desktop). Not for phones or tablets.

Play Ringfall

🦸 Hero Clash

A side-view superhero fighting game with 16 original heroes and villains, combos, special powers, and Ultimate Moves. Play the computer, a friend on the same keyboard, or a classroom tournament. No blood or graphic content.

Best with a keyboard or gamepad. Touch buttons on tablets.

Play Hero Clash

⚽ Turbo League

Drive rocket-powered cars and knock a giant ball into the goal. Train in Coach Watford's Academy, build your car in the Garage, and play the computer, a friend, or classmates online with a join code.

Keyboard, gamepad, or touch. Chromebooks: choose Low graphics if it runs slowly.

Play Turbo League
