🌧️ Featured game

Word Rain

Words fall down the chalkboard. Type each one before it lands on the ledge!

  1. Pick Easy, Medium, or Hard, then press Start.
  2. Type any falling word to clear it. Start with its first letter.
  3. Each word that lands costs one of your 5 raindrops.
  4. Clear 10 words to reach the next level. Words fall faster each level!

Esc pauses the game. Backspace lets you switch to a different word. Your best score is saved on your own device.

🖐️ The home row

ASDFJKL;

Feel for the little bumps on F and J. That's where your pointer fingers rest.

  • Sit up straight with your feet flat on the floor.
  • Keep your eyes on the screen, not your hands.
  • Accuracy first, speed second.
