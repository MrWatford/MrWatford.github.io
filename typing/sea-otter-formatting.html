🎒 Get Ready

Which computer are you using?

The shortcuts on this page will change to match your keyboard.

The golden rule

Select first, then format. If nothing changes when you click a button, you probably forgot to highlight the text.

  • Double-click a word to select just that word.
  • Triple-click to select a whole paragraph.
  • Click, then hold Shift and click somewhere else to select everything in between.

Part A Set Up and Style Your Text

1 Copy and Paste Into Your Own Document

  1. Open the Sea Otter Practice Document.
  2. Press Ctrl + A to select all the text, then Ctrl + C to copy it.
  3. Open a new blank Google Doc and press Ctrl + V to paste.
  4. Click Untitled document at the top left and rename it: Sea Otter Fact Sheet by First Last (use your real name).

Watch out: Type your real first and last name, not the words "First Last"!

2 Make an Eye-Catching Title

  1. Select the title The Amazing Sea Otter.
  2. Change the font size to 26 and make it bold.
  3. Change the text color to any dark color you like (the A button with the color bar).
  4. Center the title on the page.

Shortcut: Ctrl + Shift + E = Center

Tip: Click the number in the font size box, type 26, and press Enter. Remember which color you picked, because you'll use it again in Step 6.

3 Add Your Name and a Line

  1. Click at the end of Created by: and type your first and last name.
  2. Make that whole line italic and center it.
  3. Click at the end of your name, press Enter, and go to Insert > Horizontal line.

Shortcut: Ctrl + I = Italic

Watch out: Put a space after the colon before your name: "Created by: Your Name".

4 Change the Font and Line Spacing

  1. Select everything from "Sea otters are some of..." down to the very end.
  2. Change the font to Verdana and the size to 12.
  3. Go to Format > Line & paragraph spacing and choose 1.5.

Tip: Click right before "Sea otters are some of...", scroll to the bottom, then hold Shift and click after the last word. Don't select the title or your name line!

5 Bold, Italic, Underline, and Highlight

  1. In the first paragraph, make the word playful bold.
  2. Make the scientific name Enhydra lutris italic. (Scientific names are always written in italics!)
  3. Underline the words kelp forests.
  4. Highlight the last sentence of the paragraph ("God designed...") in yellow using the highlighter pen button.

Shortcuts: Ctrl + B = Bold, Ctrl + I = Italic, Ctrl + U = Underline

Tip: Double-click "playful" to grab just that one word. Only change the words listed, not the whole sentence.

Part B Organize Your Fact Sheet

6 Turn the Section Titles Into Headings

  1. Select Fun Facts. In the style box (it says "Normal text"), choose Heading 2.
  2. Do the same for How a Sea Otter Eats a Clam and Where Sea Otters Live.
  3. Change all three headings to the same color as your title.

Tip: Your title's color will show up in the "Custom" row at the bottom of the color menu, so it's easy to match.

7 Make a Bulleted List

  1. Select the five facts under Fun Facts (from "Sea otters have the thickest fur..." to "...when they dive underwater.").
  2. Click the Bulleted list button so each fact has a bullet point.

Shortcut: Ctrl + Shift + 8 = Bulleted list

Watch out: Don't select the heading "Fun Facts". It should not get a bullet.

8 Cut and Paste a Fact to Its Correct Spot

  1. Scroll to the last line of the document that starts with MOVE ME:. Delete the words "MOVE ME:".
  2. Select the rest of that sentence and press Ctrl + X to cut it.
  3. Click at the end of the last bullet in Fun Facts, press Enter, then press Ctrl + V. It should become bullet number six!
  4. Delete any empty line left behind at the bottom.

Shortcut: Ctrl + X = Cut (it removes the text so you can move it)

Watch out: Use Cut, not Copy. If the sentence is still at the bottom, you copied it instead of cutting it.

9 Make a Numbered List

  1. Select the six steps under How a Sea Otter Eats a Clam.
  2. Click the Numbered list button. The steps should be numbered 1 through 6.

Shortcut: Ctrl + Shift + 7 = Numbered list

Tip: If your list doesn't start at 1, right-click the first number and choose Restart numbering.

Part C Add a Picture

10 Insert and Resize a Picture

  1. Click at the end of the first paragraph and press Enter.
  2. Go to Insert > Image > Search the web and type sea otter. Pick a school-appropriate picture and click Insert.
  3. Click the picture. Drag a corner square to resize it so it is about half the width of the page. (Corners keep the picture from getting stretched!)
  4. With the picture selected, center it on the page.

📨 Turn It In!

  1. Check that all 10 steps have a check mark.
  2. Go to File > Email > Email this file.
  3. To: Mr. Watford's email (he will give it to you in class).

How it's graded: 1 point for each step, 10 points total. The bonus is worth +1 extra credit.

Pick a light color so the words are still easy to read!
