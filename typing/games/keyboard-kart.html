Ready to race?

Start your engines! Keep your fingers on the home row. Your typing is your gas pedal!
How to play
  • Type the white word to step on the gas. Keep typing to keep your speed!
  • Drive through a ? box for a power-up, then type the yellow word to use it.
  • Oil ahead! Type the red word fast to hop over it.
  • Type 10 words in a row with no mistakes for a 🔥 streak boost.
  • Finish 3 laps and try to beat all 3 racers.
  • Think you're fast? Try the 👑 Boss Race against Mr. Watford!
  • Backspace switches words. Esc pauses.
Power-ups
  • 🚀 Turbo: a big burst of speed.
  • 🛡️ Shield: blocks the next oil slick, banana, or shell.
  • ❄️ Freeze Ray: slows down every other racer.
  • 🍌 Banana Peel: drops behind you. Racers who hit it spin out!
  • 🧲 Magnet: pulls you toward the leader.
  • 🟢 Green Bouncer: flies straight ahead and knocks out the first racer it reaches (if it gets there in time).
  • 🔴 Red Seeker: chases down the racer right in front of you.
  • 🔵 Blue Zapper: flies all the way to whoever is in 1st place.
  • ☄️ Rocket Cannon: only for last place! Blasts you forward and bumps everyone you pass.
  • ⚠️ Watch out! The other racers (and Mr. Watford) use items too. A Shield blocks anything, and a banana you're holding blocks a Green or Red shell from behind. Hop their bananas like oil.
