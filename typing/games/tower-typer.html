Defend the castle!

Monsters are marching on the castle! Type their words and I'll zap them with lightning. Ready, wizard?
How to play
  • Type a monster's word to zap it.
  • Click a stone pad to build a tower. Click a tower to upgrade or sell it.
  • Click the castle to upgrade your wizard powers.
  • Type meteor, freeze, or heal to cast a spell when you have enough mana.
  • Clean words in a row build your combo for bonus coins.
  • Backspace erases a letter. Enter starts the next wave early. Esc pauses.
