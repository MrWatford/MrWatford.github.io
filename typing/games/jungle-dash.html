How to play
  1. Press ⬅ ➡ (or A and D) any time to move between the 3 lanes. Steer around boulders and grab coins!
  2. Logs, rivers, and branches have a word. Type it before you get there and your explorer jumps or ducks.
  3. Too slow, or ran into a boulder? You trip, and the gorilla catches up! Trip twice while he's close and you're caught.
  4. Type the gold words to grab power-ups: 🛡️ Vine Shield, 🦥 Sloth Time, 🧲 Coin Magnet, ⚡ Jungle Dash, and 🍌 Banana Toss.
  5. Backspace switches words. Esc pauses.
