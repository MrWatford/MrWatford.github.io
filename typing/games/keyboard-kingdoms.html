Defend your castle. Type to call your army!

Mr. Watford says: "Type a card's word to send it into battle. Knock down the red towers before they knock down yours!"
How to play
  1. Your 4 cards are below the arena. Type a card's word to play it.
  2. Each card costs 💧 mana. Mana fills up over time. Big cards have longer words!
  3. Press ⬅ ➡ (or A and D) to pick the left or right bridge.
  4. Destroy a red tower for a 👑 crown. Knock down the King's tower to win right away!
  5. Type the gold 💧 mana word when it pops up for +2 mana.
  6. Most crowns after 3 minutes wins. Backspace switches words. Esc pauses.
