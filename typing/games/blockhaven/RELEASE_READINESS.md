# Leviathan Expansion release readiness

**Full expansion: in development; not ready for a finished-release claim.** **Leviathan Early Access 1** is published with unfinished features disclosed. The owner has now authorized the scoped Dune combat update as Early Access 2; see EARLY_ACCESS_2_RELEASE.md. Subsequent development remains local until another publication request. EARLY_ACCESS_RELEASE.md records that narrower publication scope. No completion percentage or full-release date is justified yet. Approved artwork and focused passing checks do not establish completion of the full expansion.

The complete requirements are in LEVIATHAN_EXPANSION.md. IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md contains the feature-by-feature status and historical evidence. This shorter checklist records the remaining release gates.
