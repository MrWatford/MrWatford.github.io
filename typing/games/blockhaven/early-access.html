BlockHaven · Early Access 2

The sands are waking.

The Leviathan expansion is available as a work in progress. Explore its current content now; more gameplay, visual polish and testing will follow.

Play BlockHaven

New in Early Access 2

  • The Dune Leviathan's sweeping body and devouring jaws now damage where the animated creature attacks. Watch the gold warnings and move clear before the strike.
  • Its sand vortex slows movement and deals repeated damage. Leave the amber ring or use solid cover. Sand Warrior armor reduces the drag; the full set prevents it.
  • Storm attraction now clears correctly after submerged, lava and dimension exits. The storm-to-victory encounter, rewards and save/reload have passed a controlled playthrough.

Available to explore

  • Desert storms, new desert creatures and early encounters with three legendary Leviathans.
  • Leviathan bonding and riding systems, legendary equipment and mount abilities.
  • Desert landmarks, temple extensions, lore and the underground Hollow Sands realm.
  • Animated creature models, clearer danger markers and melee/projectile targeting along the bosses' bodies.

Still being developed

  • Some boss attacks and phases, fight balance and taming/progression paths need further work and complete playthrough testing.
  • Some structures, puzzles, environments, creature behavior and visual details are unfinished.
  • Area attacks need further checks against the long boss bodies. Performance across devices and compatibility with older worlds are still being tested.
Keep a copy of important worlds.

Worlds are stored in your browser on this device. Use Download in the world list or pause menu to save a backup before trying early-access content. A fresh world is the best place to experiment while older-world testing continues.

Getting started

Create a Survival world, explore desert terrain and watch for storm warnings. Press J to open the journal and look for the Leviathan tab. Keyboard and mouse or trackpad are required; use Fast graphics and a shorter view distance if play feels slow.

Current saves stay at the same game address. Early access does not mean that every feature in the expansion design is complete or release-tested.
