The Leviathan Expansion

Run through a desert sandstorm, investigate ancient temples and descend into the Hollow Sands to face the Ancient Leviathan.

Play BlockHaven

Find the three Leviathans

  1. Dune Leviathan. Sprint on open desert sand during the day to stir up a storm. Sprint again once the storm reaches full strength to draw the Dune out.
  2. Obsidian Leviathan. Use the Explorer’s Map to find the Obsidian Citadel. Cross the basalt causeway and enter the inner court. Watch for lava and heated ground.
  3. Ancient Leviathan. Follow the Desert Shrine and temple clues to earn the Ancient Desert Core. Use it to open the Sealed Gateway, read the six records in Hollow Sands, then bring the Core to the Buried Throne.

Press M for the map and J for the Leviathan journal. In Survival, choose Easy, Normal or Hard to meet the bosses.

Earn equipment and ride a Leviathan

Boss encounters unlock legendary weapons and armor. Follow the journal’s bonding rites for the Dune and Obsidian Leviathans. Use a Sandcaller Horn to recall a bonded mount and a saddle to ride it. In Creative, use the Leviathan eggs to preview all three bosses and practice riding the two mountable ones.

Explore beyond the arena

Discover the Great Dune Observatory, Desert Shrine and Buried Royal Tomb. The temple’s glyph puzzles and traps lead toward the Sealed Gateway. Below it are six regions with their own hazards, creatures and records. Some added structures appear in newly generated layouts.

Back up worlds you want to keep.

Your worlds stay at the same game address. Choose Download in the world list or pause menu to keep a backup on your device.

Play with a keyboard and mouse or trackpad. Use Fast graphics and a shorter view distance if play feels slow. Reduced motion is available in Settings.
