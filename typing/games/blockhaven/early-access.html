The sands are waking.

The Leviathan expansion is available as a work in progress. Explore its current content now; more gameplay, visual polish and testing will follow.

Play BlockHaven

New in Early Access 4

  • Much more frequent desert storms. About eight seconds of grounded daytime sprinting on open desert sand can stir one; a fight with three nearby hostile mobs can also awaken the weather. Walking quietly avoids stirring another storm.
  • Called storms build for thirty seconds before their strongest phase, hold that phase for a minute, then fade and settle. Weather alone does not create a Leviathan: fast footsteps during the peak still draw it out.
  • Read the shrine, ready all three relics and craft a Sandcaller Horn to call a storm at the open Observatory instrument. Its forecast explains buildup and cooldown; existing bonded-companion horn controls retain priority.
  • The journal explains the horn recipe and material sources, tracks each relic and keeps shrine bearings. Shrine reading, relic caches and an earned Dune bond checkpoint their progress.
  • The Ancient Leviathan has persistent blue ground warnings for judgment, its awakening and guardian arrivals, with readable countdowns and reduced-motion support. Guardians arrive at their marked centers and respect space and population limits.
  • Ancient awakening, newly reached combat phases and victory save immediately. New victories keep all six original treasure stacks and 350 experience safe at the Throne altar until the full set fits in your backpack. Repeated claims cannot duplicate rewards; older completed worlds keep their existing reward status.

Available to explore

  • Desert storms, new desert creatures and early encounters with three legendary Leviathans.
  • Leviathan bonding and riding systems, legendary equipment and mount abilities.
  • Desert landmarks, temple extensions, lore and the underground Hollow Sands realm.
  • Animated creature models, clearer danger markers and melee/projectile targeting along the bosses' bodies.

Still being developed

  • The full Ancient awakening presentation, phase/finale work, fight balance and natural progression still need further work. Controlled acceptance checks do not replace complete unassisted fights, death/retry routes or mixed-enemy testing.
  • Some structures, puzzles, environments, creature behavior and visual details are unfinished.
  • Area attacks need further checks against the long boss bodies. Performance across devices and compatibility with older worlds are still being tested.
Keep a copy of important worlds.

Worlds are stored in your browser on this device. Use Download in the world list or pause menu to save a backup before trying early-access content. A fresh world is the best place to experiment while older-world testing continues.

Getting started

Create a Survival world, explore desert terrain and watch for storm warnings. Press J to open the journal and look for the Leviathan tab. Keyboard and mouse or trackpad are required; use Fast graphics and a shorter view distance if play feels slow.

If the title still shows an earlier version, refresh the page before playing.

Current saves stay at the same game address. Early access does not mean that every feature in the expansion design is complete or release-tested.
