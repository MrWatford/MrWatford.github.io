The Lost World

Play all eight chapters of the Lost World expedition, alongside the Survival and Creative sandbox. Continue beyond the Redwood Escarpment into the Amber Caverns, Tyrant Canyon, Volcanic Badlands, and the Caldera.

Open New World and choose The Lost World (Campaign) or The Lost World (Sandbox) under World terrain. Press J to review objectives or travel between regions. Your existing worlds remain in the Play menu.

Face Godzilla

Restore all three Badlands anchors to reach the final chapter. Godzilla stands about 14 blocks tall and fights through five phases. Take gear from the Caldera supply chest, then use the entrance terminal. Dodge the orange eruption rings and blue atomic-breath lane, strike during recovery, and ground the three arena anchors during Broken Circuit.

Godzilla and the Tyrannosaurus now have detailed voxel bodies, articulated jaws, teeth, claws, and tapered tails. Godzilla’s dorsal plates and mouth glow as he charges, and atomic breath follows his head. Low effects keeps the attack lane and beam visible with the outer glow turned off.

This is a playable preview. Campaign and boss balance are still being refined. Co-op and performance on lower-end devices need further testing.

Wildlife Expansion

Discover all 27 wildlife species in Survival, along with the rare Ancient Grizzly variant. Press J and choose Wildlife to track what you see.

Play BlockHaven

Where to look

  • Forests and mountains. Watch for Bald Eagles overhead and give Grizzly Bears, their dens, and their cubs room.
  • Open water and the seabed. Find Great White Sharks, Blue Whales, Orcas, Octopuses, and Manta Rays.
  • Riverbanks and cold shores. Watch for Crocodiles and Hippos by the water, and look for Seal and Walrus colonies along cold beaches.
  • Warm grasslands. Look for Lions, Zebras, Giraffes, Rhinos, Ostriches, Kangaroos and Elephant herds. Falcons patrol plains and cliffs.
  • Woodlands and forest edges. Look for Tigers, Deer, Moose, Red Pandas, Sloths, Koalas and Peafowl.
  • Warm shallows. Flamingos wade, feed and rest in flocks.

All 27 species have spawn eggs in the Creative inventory under Creatures. The Ancient Grizzly is a rare den variant of the Grizzly Bear.

Train a falcon or befriend an elephant

Offer raw rabbit or chicken to a resting falcon. Once trained, crouch and interact to send it scouting ahead. Offer an adult elephant wheat or apples, with a short pause between offerings, to earn its trust. Equip a Saddle to ride, or crouch and interact to open its cargo.

The Leviathan Expansion

Run through a desert sandstorm, investigate ancient temples and descend into the Hollow Sands to face the Ancient Leviathan.

Find the three Leviathans

  1. Dune Leviathan. Sprint on open desert sand during the day to stir up a storm. Sprint again once the storm reaches full strength to draw the Dune out.
  2. Obsidian Leviathan. Use the Explorer’s Map to find the Obsidian Citadel. Cross the basalt causeway and enter the inner court. Watch for lava and heated ground.
  3. Ancient Leviathan. Follow the Desert Shrine and temple clues to earn the Ancient Desert Core. Use it to open the Sealed Gateway, read the six records in Hollow Sands, then bring the Core to the Buried Throne.

Press M for the map and J for the Leviathan journal. In Survival, choose Easy, Normal or Hard to meet the bosses.

Earn equipment and ride a Leviathan

Boss encounters unlock legendary weapons and armor. Follow the journal’s bonding rites for the Dune and Obsidian Leviathans. Use a Sandcaller Horn to recall a bonded mount and a saddle to ride it. In Creative, use the Leviathan eggs to preview all three bosses and practice riding the two mountable ones.

Explore beyond the arena

Discover the Great Dune Observatory, Desert Shrine and Buried Royal Tomb. The temple’s glyph puzzles and traps lead toward the Sealed Gateway. Below it are six regions with their own hazards, creatures and records. Some added structures appear in newly generated layouts.

Back up worlds you want to keep.

Your worlds stay at the same game address. Choose Download in the world list or pause menu to keep a backup on your device.

Play with a keyboard and mouse or trackpad. Use Fast graphics and a shorter view distance if play feels slow. Reduced motion is available in Settings.
