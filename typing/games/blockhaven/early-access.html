BlockHaven · Early Access 5

The sands are waking.

The Leviathan expansion is available as a work in progress. Explore its current content now; more gameplay, visual polish and testing will follow.

Play BlockHaven

New in Early Access 5

  • The Ancient Leviathan rises gradually during its seven-second awakening, with falling ceiling sand, charging blue runes and an emergence roar pose. Reduced motion keeps essential warnings while hiding decorative sand.
  • Ancient Fury locks three rune strikes before they land. Final Judgment marks its two temporary collapse sites, and the defeated King sinks and fades beneath the arena.
  • Attacks retain the damage and hazards they warned about, even when a hit pushes the boss into its next phase.
  • Runecrest checkpoints its health, healing, Sit/Follow preference and summon/retreat state. Wounded retreat keeps its existing one-minute recovery.
  • Controlled checks now cover one connected five-phase Ancient encounter, death and retry, dropped-equipment recovery, full-backpack treasure claims and Hardcore reload/import outcomes. Spectator players no longer attract or collect dropped items.

Earlier improvements remain included

Frequent desert storms and daytime activity triggers, the earned Observatory storm call, shrine/relic guidance, guardian arrival markers and safe one-time Ancient treasure claims remain available.

Available to explore

  • Desert storms, new desert creatures and early encounters with three legendary Leviathans.
  • Leviathan bonding and riding systems, legendary equipment and mount abilities.
  • Desert landmarks, temple extensions, lore and the underground Hollow Sands realm.
  • Animated creature models, clearer danger markers and melee/projectile targeting along the bosses' bodies.

Still being developed

  • Full cinematic/music presentation, fight balance and natural progression still need further work. Controlled encounter and recovery checks do not replace complete unassisted fights or mixed-enemy testing.
  • Some structures, puzzles, environments, creature behavior and visual details are unfinished.
  • Area attacks need further checks against the long boss bodies. Performance across devices and compatibility with older worlds are still being tested.
Keep a copy of important worlds.

Worlds are stored in your browser on this device. Use Download in the world list or pause menu to save a backup before trying early-access content. A fresh world is the best place to experiment while older-world testing continues.

Getting started

Create a Survival world, explore desert terrain and watch for storm warnings. Press J to open the journal and look for the Leviathan tab. Keyboard and mouse or trackpad are required; use Fast graphics and a shorter view distance if play feels slow.

If the title still shows an earlier version, refresh the page before playing.

Current saves stay at the same game address. Early access does not mean that every feature in the expansion design is complete or release-tested.
