## Constraints and design decisions to validate

- Block IDs occupy eight bits of a packed voxel. Definitions currently reach 210, with at most 45 higher IDs available through 255. Audit actual free IDs, atlas tiles, item IDs, spawn eggs, map encoders and metadata bits before allocation. Prefer existing palette blocks or compatible variants where they preserve the art direction. A wider voxel format would need its own compatibility milestone.
- Generation is currently version 8. A chunk-level freshness check alone cannot protect a multi-chunk structure. Reserve and validate the full footprint against visited chunks, edits, containers, authored stamps and existing landmark plans before committing placement. Persist selected sites and versioned plans, including negative coordinates and deterministic fallback sites. Extend the existing temple through a compatible annex with its own version and safe entry, subject to validating the current routes and saves.
- One simulation root per boss and a shared path sampled by visual segments. Use a few bounded collision volumes, distance-based animation detail, pooled effects and explicit cleanup. Never load every chunk a long visual tail crosses. Track frame time and chunk rebuilds with storm, boss and traversal active together.
- Use visual sand displacement and capped hazards. Existing griefing settings are only one guard. Protect homes, storage, structures and progression blocks even when griefing is enabled. Surface and underground travel must have valid exits and keep the player clear of unloaded or solid terrain.
- Persist unique encounter and bond identity independently of a loaded mesh or chunk. Choose one authoritative record and reconcile materialized entities on load. Grant rewards with durable progress and a recoverable pending claim when inventory is full. Test interrupted saves, repeated input and duplicate portal loads. Confirm older imports and version handling before advancing save format.
- Resolve progression ambiguities in Milestone 1, including combat versus taming access, recovery of lost quest items, and which progression flags Creative helpers may affect. Avoid gating one boss, relic, portal or recipe behind an item that first becomes available through that same gate. Preserve existing Hollow, Echo Expanse and Lost Civilization terminology and progression alongside Solkara.
