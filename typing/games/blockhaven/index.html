Blockhaven: The Legacy Campaign

An eight-chapter adventure in a world called The Forgotten Kingdoms. It starts in Legacy Village, with its castle, its people, and a story about eight lost Relics. It is still a real sandbox: mine, build, and farm wherever you like. Campaign saves are kept apart from your other worlds.

Start a new campaign

All eight chapters are ready, right up to the final choice and the credits. Starting at a later chapter gives you the gear and Relics from the chapters before it. Start at Chapter 1 and play to the end without skipping to earn The Whole Story. You can switch chapters any time from the Campaign tab of your journal (J).

Hollow Sands: leave blue crystal rings before they fill and release energy. Gold swirls mark soft Abyssal sand: keep moving or press Space to jump free of its extra drag. Orange cracks build heat: step clear or use Fire Resistance.

Inventory: click a stack to pick it up and click a slot to put it down. Right-click picks up half or drops one. Shift-click moves a stack across. Point at a slot and press 1–9 to swap it into the hotbar.

Two hands: your offhand (the ✋ slot in the inventory, shown left of the hotbar) holds a second item. Right-click uses your main hand first; if it has nothing to do, the offhand acts. Left-click always uses the main hand. Try a sword in your main hand and a Shield in your offhand: hold right-click to block hits from the front. A Lifeward Totem in either hand saves you once from fainting (not from the void). Look for totems in treasure chests, or buy one from a Wanderer.

Food: eating takes a moment, so hold right-click. Each food fills the food bar and also gives hidden fullness that gets used up first. Filling foods (steak, cooked porkchops, golden carrots) keep you healing longer. Sprinting, jumping, swimming, fighting, mining, and healing use up fullness; walking doesn't. You can't sprint with 3 or fewer drumsticks. Hover over a food in your inventory to compare.

Void Chest: craft one from 8 obsidian and an Eye of the Void. Every Void Chest opens the same personal storage, in every dimension.

Devouring strike: sidestep the glowing mouth before it bites. Dune tail sweep: clear the glowing body segments before they sweep; solid cover can protect you. Dune vortex: leave the amber ring or take cover behind a solid wall. The swirling sand slows movement and deals repeated damage until it disperses. Two Sand Warrior pieces reduce the drag; the full set prevents it.

Survival: keep your hearts and food full. Eat by holding food and right-clicking. Monsters come out in the dark, so light your home with torches. Sleep in a bed to skip the night and set where you wake up. If you faint, you wake up at your bed (or the starting spot). You keep your items unless that world option is turned off.

The Ember Depths: build an obsidian frame (at least 4 wide and 5 tall, corners optional) and light it with Flint and Steel. Stand in the portal for a moment to travel. Water boils away there, beds explode, and Boarkin leave you alone if you wear gold. Hand them gold ingots to trade. One step there equals 8 steps on the Surface.

The Hollow: craft an Eye of the Void (Void Pearl + Flare Powder) and right-click to throw it. It flies toward a stronghold, so follow it. When it dives into the ground, dig down. Put eyes in all 12 frames to open the portal. In Creative you can build your own ring from Hollow Portal Frames: 3 frames on each side of a 3×3 space, no corners, all at the same height. In the Hollow, shoot the crystals on the pillars, then defeat the Wyrm (it takes the most damage while it rests in the middle). Afterward, a Gateway leads to the outer islands, where Hollow towers hide Glider Wings: wear them as a chestplate and press Space while falling to glide.

The final dragon: after you defeat the Hollow Wyrm it gives you a Cinder Eye (or craft one from a Wyrm Scale, an Eye of the Void, Magma Cream, and 2 Flare Powder). Throw it in the Ember Depths and follow it to the Cinder Citadel. Charge the Respawn Anchor in its entrance hall, then put the eye in the Cinder Altar to wake Cindermaw, the Ember Dragon. Watch for its warnings: a roar before it dives, glowing rings before fire, spread wings before a gust (hide behind a pillar). Hit it hardest when it lands. In its second phase, break the glowing Ember Pylons to remove its shield.

The Forsaken World (optional): find Pale Shards in Ember Depths fortresses (chests and Blight Rattlers). 4 shards + Shinestone + 2 iron ingots make a Pale Lantern: right-click it on the Surface to enter Herobrine's valley. You start in a safe refuge with a Return Gate home. Inside a ward's ring you're always safe. Running, mining, and fighting draw his attention; walking and crouching don't. The eye in the corner shows the real danger. Read the notes, restore the three shrines with their sigils, and face him in his citadel. Press J for your journal.

Villages: villagers work at their job blocks by day and go home at night. Right-click one to trade with Jade (found in mountain caves, chests, or earned by selling to villagers). The Ironwarden guards each village, so don't hurt the villagers! A Wanderer sometimes visits with rare goods.

Pets and riding: tame a Scout (grey, in forests) by giving it bones, and a Whisker (ginger, near villages and in grassland) with raw chicken or raw rabbit. It may take a few tries. Pets follow you, Scouts fight monsters near you, and Hissers stay away from Whiskers. Right-click a pet to make it sit or follow. To tame a wild Trotter (grassland), right-click it with an empty hand to climb on until it stops bucking you off (feeding it wheat or apples helps). Then craft a Saddle (5 leather + 1 iron ingot), right-click the Trotter with it, and ride: Shift gallops, Space jumps high, and C gets you off.

The Blightking: stack 4 Wailing Sand in a T and place 3 Blight Skulls on top. Back away! It explodes when it wakes. Below half health its armor stops arrows, so finish it with a sword. Its Radiant Star crafts a Beacon that heals you and speeds you up nearby.

Water: craft a Boat (5 planks at a workbench), right-click water to set it down, then right-click it to climb in. A Fishing Rod (3 sticks + 2 string) catches fish: right-click to cast, wait for the bobber to dip under with a splash, then right-click quickly to reel in. Don't eat Puffers! Swim near a friendly Splasher to swim faster, or feed it a minnow. Inklings drop Ink Sacs (Wool + Ink Sac = Black Wool).

Wildlife: Buzzers carry pollen from flowers to their nest. When honey drips from the front, use a Glass Bottle (honey cures poison) or Shears (Honeycomb makes a Beehive). Put a Torch within 3 blocks first, or the bees get angry! Pouncers nap by day, hunt chickens at night, and run off unless you crouch. Feed two of them Sweet Berries for a baby that trusts you. Tame a Chatterbird with wheat seeds and it rides on your shoulder (crouch to let it hop off).

Creatures: left-click to attack. Hold a bow's right-click to pull, then let go to shoot. Hold wheat, carrots, or seeds to lead animals, and right-click two of the same kind to breed them. Use shears on sheep and a bucket on cows.
