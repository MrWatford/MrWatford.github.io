Ordinary creature boxes and ordinary mounted-creature behavior are unchanged; gone entities are no longer projectile candidates.

## Swept cover and nearest contacts

`js/projectile-collision.js` now checks the existing quarter-block projectile substep as a closed segment. It examines both sides of exact voxel planes and one row below for 1.5-block fences/gates. Actual block collision shapes preserve doorway, trapdoor and fence openings. Contacts only at an endpoint moving away from a face are ignored. Stuck arrows use the exact impact position and block callbacks receive that position and the contacted cell.

The entity loop now finds the nearest intersection rather than the first entity in list order. Terrain, eligible creatures and the player are compared within the same substep, with terrain winning ties. Pearls still ignore creatures; existing enemy-arrow friendly-fire and armored interception behavior remain. Damage values, ammunition, durability, invulnerability, projectile expiry and cleanup retain their existing rules. The root-box combat contract remains unchanged; this does not add body-segment targets or resize the approved creature.

The scan bounds are twelve candidate cells per existing <=0.25-block substep, including the lower fence/gate row. Fence links can add four neighbor reads per candidate, at most sixty terrain reads. The helper reuses a shape-array container and a manager-owned impact record. Shape boxes and existing Three.js projectile meshes retain their normal ownership. The collision code neither loads chunks nor writes terrain. Compared with endpoint-only checks this increases terrain-query work and scans all eligible creatures to establish nearest order; dense-projectile hardware profiling remains required.

Twenty focused groups passed: thirteen cover/shape/ordering/lifecycle groups and all seven prior boss projectile groups. Tests include positive/negative grid planes and corners, all three axes/both directions, interior/departing contacts, closed/open doors, trapdoors, gates and tall fences, read limits, exact callback coordinates, creatures before/behind cover, reversed entity insertion order, player/creature order, hostile rune bolts, pearls, enemy friendly-fire and eight explosive/breath/shrapnel/thrown-item paths. Evidence is `tools/review-results/2026-11-07-projectile-cover.json`.

The actual game fixture passed nine groups: its six existing bow/damage/storage groups plus exact X/Y edge cover, thin closed/open doors and a creature immediately behind cover within one substep. Evidence is `tools/review-results/2026-11-07-live-projectile-cover.json`. Settings and paused sky-corridor limitations match the initial review. After the original terrain-preservation/save checks, the cover fixture intentionally authors one temporary sky cell in its named disposable world, then restores it to air; the explicit air edit remains. Final game and focused tabs captured no warnings/errors. No production terrain or deployment was changed. The expansion remains unfinished and unpublished.

## Animated direct-hit geometry

Wild Dune, Obsidian and Ancient bosses now expose thirteen existing meshes to both melee and direct projectiles: the head shell, two jaws and ten body cores. Each mesh has a cached inverse world transform and shared read-only geometry/bounds. Transforms refresh after animation, so turning, head pitch/roll, body curves and species scale are reflected in targeting. Local box rejection precedes triangle intersections; at most 440 existing triangles are available across the thirteen meshes, without adding rendered geometry. Decorative ridges, flanks, fangs and maw rim plates are not separate damage surfaces. The head's actual recessed throat and geometry gaps remain meaningful.

Triangle-edge tolerance is 1e-8 in local barycentric/contact calculations, with normalized local rays and tiny broad-box expansion to avoid hairline numerical gaps on scaled/turning rigs. Independent off-seam Three.js mesh comparisons cover 1,872 rays across all three variants, four attack/turn states and both motion settings. Twenty-four analytical exact head-seam checks supplement that reference, which itself is sensitive to exact triangle-edge rays. Actor/ancestor visibility, hidden LOD parts, burial, death, dismissal and riding exclude surfaces. Ordinary creatures and bonded interaction targeting retain their existing boxes. Cached CPU references clear on entity removal and are absent from serialization.

The nearest surface resolves to one entity, preserving ordinary damage, invulnerability, knockback exclusion and authoritative story HP. A projectile crossing overlapping parts still deals one hit. Area/radius abilities and explosions continue to use existing root-based queries; physical movement and attacks against the player are unchanged. No model dimensions, texture, geometry, lights, new save fields or terrain changes were introduced by this collision feature. Thirteen transform inversions per animated boss and bounded triangle work replace the old single-box query; crowded-fight hardware profiling is still required.

Thirty-seven focused groups passed: ten rig/contact groups, thirteen cover groups, seven boss-projectile groups and seven animation groups. The animation regression includes 1,440 normal/reduced-motion frames. The clean final run captured no warnings/errors.
