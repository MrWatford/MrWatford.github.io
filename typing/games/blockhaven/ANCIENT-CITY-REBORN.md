## 2. Limits found (these change parts of the notes)

1. **The world is 80 blocks tall.** The notes ask for a ceiling 80–160 blocks above the city, but the whole engine (chunk arrays, saves, meshing) uses 80. Raising it would touch every world and every dimension, so it's too risky. **Plan:** keep 80. The city floor sits low (about y 10), the cavern reaches about y 75, and tall hanging stalactites, chains and dark fog at the top make the ceiling feel endless.
2. **Fog hides anything past about 55 blocks** (render distance 4, which Chromebooks need). A 500-block city can't be seen all at once. **Plan:** a "far city" backdrop: one cheap mesh of building and tower outlines drawn beyond the fog, so the Grand Entrance overlook shows the whole city as dark shapes with lit windows. It costs about as much as the sky.
3. **Light is always warm orange.** The teal glow in his pictures needs a second light color. **Plan:** add a "cool light" channel to the mesher (hushmoss, crystals, runes, soul lamps), next to the warm one (torches, lanterns). This is the biggest graphics change. It applies everywhere, so it has to be tested on the Surface and in the Ember Depths too.
4. **No bloom or post-processing.** Full-screen effects are slow on Chromebooks. **Plan:** fake the glow with small soft sprites around bright blocks (one batch), on Medium/High only.
5. **The released map `deep-v1.bin` must never be changed** (a project rule). Worlds that already visited the Deep have player edits saved on top of it. **Plan (Strategy B from the notes):** build `deep-v2.bin`. A world that has never been to the Deep (`dims.deep.tplId` empty) gets v2. A world that already has `tplId = "deep-v1"` keeps v1 exactly, with all of its edits, chests and story progress. Later, if wanted, an opt-in "Visit the new Echohold" choice could make a backup first and then switch. Nothing changes silently.
6. **Name clashes.** "Ancient" is already used by the Ancient Leviathan, the Ancient Stone Guardian and 20+ `ancient-*.js` files. New files will start with `echohold-` and new names will avoid "Ancient".
7. **Version tags.** Every new or changed module needs its `?v=` tag in the index.html import map changed before publishing (the Leviathan release rule), or returning students' browsers keep old files.
