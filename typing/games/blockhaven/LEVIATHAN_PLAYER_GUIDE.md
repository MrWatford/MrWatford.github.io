# How to Meet and Fight the Three Leviathans

For the live **BlockHaven: Leviathan Early Access 4**.

Play: https://mrwatford.github.io/typing/games/blockhaven/

## Get ready

1. Choose **Survival**. Use **Easy**, **Normal** or **Hard**. Peaceful keeps these boss fights asleep.
2. Bring armor, a sword, food and a pickaxe. Download a backup of a world you care about.
3. Press **M** for the Explorer's Map. Press **J**, then choose **Leviathan**, for your adventure checklist.
4. Start with the **Desert Shrine** marked on your map. Choose it and follow the waypoint. Press **N** if you need to show the arrow again.

Recommended adventure order: **Dune → Obsidian → Ancient**. The first two fights do not have to be completed to unlock the Ancient: its actual requirements are the Core and six underground records.

## 1. Dune Leviathan — the sandstorm boss

**Meeting place: a big, open patch of sand in the Surface desert.** It has no single home or fixed map marker.

1. Find open desert sand during the day.
2. Hold **Shift + W** to sprint for about **8 seconds**. Keep your feet on the sand; use a clear route.
3. When the storm starts gathering, give it about **30 seconds** to build to its strongest stage.
4. Sprint across the open sand again for at least **3 seconds**. Keep open sand ahead of you.
5. Watch for the message that something is tracking your footsteps. The Leviathan will rise nearby!
6. Move away from its ground warnings. Hit its visible head or body with your sword when it is above the sand. Keep moving and eat when you need to.

**Other ways to get a storm:** fight three nearby hostile mobs outdoors in the daytime, or wait for natural weather. Later, read the shrine, attune all three relics, craft the Sandcaller Horn and use it beside the Observatory instrument to call a storm.

**For this fight, you can start without a horn or relics.** Those are needed for the bonding rite and the earned Observatory call.

If nothing happens, check daylight, Survival and difficulty. Find flatter open sand, keep moving fast and wait until the storm is at its strongest. A defeated or bonded Dune Leviathan will not start another wild fight in that world.

## 2. Obsidian Leviathan — the volcanic boss

**Meeting place: the inner court of the Obsidian Citadel in the Surface desert.** Its location changes between worlds.

1. Explore the desert for the **Obsidian Citadel**: a dark fortress with volcanic features.
2. Once you discover it, check its marker on **M**. Undiscovered landmarks are not all shown on the map.
3. Find and read **The Basalt Watch** tablet near the Citadel entrance. It tells you the inner court's X and Z coordinates.
4. Follow the dark stone causeway across the dangerous outer ring. Stay off the burning ground.
5. Walk into the middle of the inner court, on the arena floor. The message about the Citadel trembling means the boss is waking!
6. Leave the orange ground warnings, move away from its shrapnel paths and use cover. Attack when it surfaces and recovers.

**To begin a fight, entering the inner court is enough.** The Dune bond, horn and offering are extra requirements for taming the Obsidian Leviathan, not for fighting it.

If it does not wake, check that you are inside the inner court rather than on the outer wall, and at floor level. Peaceful, Creative, or an already defeated/bonded Obsidian encounter will not start this wild fight.

## 3. Ancient Leviathan — the Buried King

**Meeting place: the Throne of the Buried King inside Hollow Sands.** This is the underground desert realm. The older Ancient City is a different place.

### Open the underground realm

1. Follow **M** to the Desert Shrine. Right-click its inscription to read it.
2. Open the relic caches at the **Desert Shrine**, **Leviathan Graveyard** and **Buried Royal Tomb**. Reading the shrine gives you directions in **J → Leviathan**. Opening each cache attunes its relic to you.
3. Press **J → Leviathan → Visit Solkara sanctuary**. This is the simplest route to the needed halls. You can also explore the original Desert Temple route.
4. Find the **Chamber of Echoes**. Read the mural, then use its glyph switches in this order: **Sun → River → Star**.
5. With all three relics attuned, use the **Leviathan Altar** to collect the **Ancient Desert Core**. Leave one backpack slot empty for it.
6. Use the **Sealed Gateway** once to open it. Use it again to enter **Hollow Sands**.

### Wake the boss

7. Explore the six underground regions and read the large record tablet in each: **Pale Dunes**, **Crystal Wastes**, **Bone Fields**, **Forgotten Kingdom**, **Abyssal Sands** and **Eternal Oasis**.
8. Check **J → Leviathan → The Last Sandcaller**. You need **6/6** of these records. Extra library stories do not replace these six region records.
9. Go to the **Throne of the Buried King**. The court is centered around **X 0, Z 192**. Its activation altar is at **X 0, Z 213**, on the court's floor.
10. Right-click the Throne altar. With the Core claimed and all six records read, the Ancient Leviathan will wake after a seven-second buildup!
11. Move out of its blue danger circles, watch the guardian arrival markers and attack while it is above ground. Save your food and keep a clear escape route.
12. After winning, return to the same Throne altar with room in your backpack. Use it to collect the full treasure set and **350 experience**. New victories keep unclaimed rewards safe there across reloads.

If the altar says it remembers fewer than six records, keep exploring the six regions. Simply carrying a Core from Creative does not replace claiming it from the Leviathan Altar.

## Remember

- **Dune:** make a strong desert storm, then sprint on open sand.
- **Obsidian:** enter the Citadel's inner court.
- **Ancient:** claim the Core, read six underground records, then use the Throne altar.
- **Fighting and taming are different paths.** The Ancient Leviathan cannot be tamed; its victory rewards include the small Runecrest companion relic.

This guide describes the published EA4 game. Later local development changes are not assumed to be live.
