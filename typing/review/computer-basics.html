Parts of a computer

PartWhat it does
KeyboardType letters and numbers.
MousePoint and click on things on the screen.
MonitorThe screen that shows your work.
SpeakersPlay sound.
USB flash driveStores files so you can carry them to another computer.
Power / batteryEvery computer needs power from a plug or a charged battery to work.

Getting online

WordMeaning
InternetThe network that connects computers around the world. You need it to visit websites.
Wi-FiConnects to the internet without a cable.
Web browserThe program you use to visit websites, like Google Chrome.
wwwWorld Wide Web
GoogleA website for searching for information.

Working smart

  • Google Docs is the program for typing essays and reports.
  • The desktop is the main screen with icons and a background picture.
  • The cursor is the blinking line that shows where your typing goes.
  • Save often so your work doesn't get lost.
  • Deleted something? It usually goes to the Recycle Bin or Trash first.
  • Turn the computer off the right way: choose Shut Down from the menu. Don't just unplug it. Save first!
  • Keep drinks away from the keyboard. A spill can damage it.

Staying safe

  • Strong passwords are long and mix letters, numbers, and symbols. Never share yours, even with friends.
  • Pop-up ads can be tricks. Close them; don't click inside them.
  • Don't open links sent to you by strangers.
  • See something online that makes you uncomfortable? Tell a teacher or parent.
