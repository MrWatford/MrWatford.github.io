The home row

  • Left hand rests on A S D F.
  • Right hand rests on J K L ;
  • F and J have small bumps so you can find the home row without looking.
  • Thumbs rest on the space bar.

Special keys

KeyWhat it does
BackspaceErases the character to the LEFT of the cursor.
Caps LockMakes every letter a capital until you press it again.
ShiftHold it for ONE capital letter, or for the symbol on the top half of a key.
EnterStarts a new line or paragraph.
TabJumps forward, like indenting a paragraph.
Space barMakes a space. Press it with your thumb.

Why is it called QWERTY?

A standard keyboard is named after the first six letters on its top letter row: Q W E R T Y.

Tips for picturing the keyboard

  • Each row sits a little to the right of the row above it. So the key "below" E is D, and the number "above" T is 5.
  • Say the rows out loud: "Q W E R T Y U I O P", "A S D F G H J K L", "Z X C V B N M".
  • Use the finger chart to see which finger presses each key.
