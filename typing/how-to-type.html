🪑 Sit like a pro

  • Sit up straight with your back against the chair.
  • Keep both feet flat on the floor.
  • Center your body with the B key.
  • Keep your wrists up a little, not resting on the desk.
  • Curve your fingers like you are holding a small ball.

⚡ Habits that make you faster

  • Find the bumps on F and J without looking.
  • Keep your eyes on the screen, not your hands.
  • Return to the home row after every key.
  • Press the space bar with your thumb.
  • Use the Shift key on the opposite hand from the letter.
  • Accuracy first. Speed comes with practice!
