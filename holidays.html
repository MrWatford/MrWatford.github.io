💡 Good to know

  • Christmas, Easter, Independence Day, and Thanksgiving get a big animated scene on the home page. Other holidays get a smaller banner.
  • Memorial Day, Veterans Day, Patriot Day, Good Friday, and Martin Luther King Jr. Day are quiet and respectful: no fireworks or moving decorations.
  • When two holidays overlap, the holiday's own day wins, and Good Friday always wins over Easter.
  • Visitors can't turn the decorations off. They can only click “Pause animations” in the ribbon to stop the motion. Computers set to reduce motion never get moving decorations.
  • Quick preview trick: add ?holiday=christmas or ?holidayDate=2027-03-28 to the end of any page address. Use ?holiday=off to stop.
