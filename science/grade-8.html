🔬 Chapter 1 • Introduction to Science

What we'll explore

How scientists ask questions, test ideas, and reason from evidence. Then we put those skills to work on something right under our feet: soil, and what makes it able to grow plants.

Topics

  • The scientific method
  • Thinking and reasoning like a scientist
  • What soil is made of
  • Nutrients plants get from soil
  • Air and water in the soil

🌍 Chapter 2 • Foundations of Geology

What we'll explore

A trip from the crust down to the core, then the powerful events that shake and reshape the surface: earthquakes, the waves they send through the ground, and volcanoes.

Topics

  • The layers of the Earth
  • Earthquakes and how we measure them
  • Volcanoes and how they erupt

💎 Chapter 3 • Minerals & Rocks

What we'll explore

What a mineral is and how to identify one by its color, streak, hardness, and shine. Then the minerals people treasure, and the three main kinds of rock that make up the Earth.

Topics

  • What minerals are and how to identify them
  • Gems, ores, and other valuable minerals
  • Igneous, sedimentary, and metamorphic rocks

  • 🧪 Labs coming soon

🏜️ Chapter 4 • Weathering & Erosion

What we'll explore

How water, wind, ice, and even plant roots break rock apart (weathering), and how moving water, wind, and glaciers carry those pieces away (erosion) to reshape the land.

Topics

  • Mechanical and chemical weathering
  • How rivers and rain carve the land
  • Glaciers, wind, and landslides

Labs & projects

  • ⚔️ Weathering Wars: video demonstration
  • 🃏 Chapter 4 test study guide (Quizlet)

🦴 Chapter 5 • The Fossil Record

What we'll explore

What fossils are, how they form, and what they can teach us about the plants and animals God created that lived in the past.

Topics

  • Where everything came from
  • Two different views of Earth's history
  • What rock layers tell us
  • Fossils as evidence of the Flood
  • The missing in-between forms
  • Man: made in God's image

Labs & projects

  • 🫙 Fossil Record Projects: video, lab sheet & grading

🌊 Chapter 6 • A Survey of the Seas

What we'll explore

Why ocean water is salty, what makes waves, currents, and tides, what the ocean floor really looks like, and how scientists explore the deep.

Topics

  • What is in seawater
  • Waves, currents, and tides
  • Shape of the ocean floor
  • How scientists study the ocean

🌤️ Chapter 7 • The Atmosphere

What we'll explore

The blanket of air God placed around the Earth: its layers, how sunlight warms land, water, and air differently, and how that uneven heating gets the winds moving.

Topics

  • Layers and makeup of the air
  • How the Sun heats the Earth
  • Winds and global air patterns

🌧️ Chapter 8 • Water Vapor & Air Masses

What we'll explore

Where weather comes from: invisible water vapor in the air, how it turns into clouds, fog, rain, and snow, and how huge bodies of air collide to bring changes in the weather.

Topics

  • Humidity and dew point
  • Kinds of clouds and fog
  • Rain, snow, sleet, and hail
  • Air masses and fronts

Thunderstorms, lightning, tornadoes, and hurricanes: how they form and how to stay safe. Then the tools meteorologists use to measure the weather and predict what's coming.

Topics

  • Thunderstorms, lightning, and tornadoes
  • Hurricanes
  • Weather instruments and forecasting

🪐 Chapter 10 • Consider the Heavens

What we'll explore

A tour of our solar system, planet by planet, plus asteroids, comets, and meteors. Then out to the constellations, the Sun and other stars, and whole galaxies, all declaring the glory of God.

Topics

  • Our solar system
  • The inner planets
  • The outer planets
  • Asteroids, comets, and meteors
  • Constellations
  • The Sun, stars, and galaxies

Topics

  • Telescopes and other astronomy tools
  • Days, months, seasons, and calendars
  • The history of space flight
  • Orbits and satellites
