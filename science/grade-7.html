🔍 Chapter 1 • Invitation to Science

What we'll explore

How scientists study the living things God created, starting in your own backyard. We'll learn how living things are sorted into groups and practice the scientific method.

Topics

  • Studying God's living creation
  • Exploring your own backyard
  • Exploring beyond your backyard
  • Classifying living things
  • The scientific method
  • Thinking like a scientist

🌷 Chapter 2 • Plants: Provision for Life

What we'll explore

How flowers make seeds, how seeds sprout and grow, and how leaves make food from sunlight. Plus how water travels through a plant, and plants that grow without flowers.

Topics

  • Flowers and their design
  • From pollen to seed
  • How a seed grows up
  • Flowers of meadow and garden
  • Monocot plant families
  • Observing leaves
  • Photosynthesis and respiration
  • How water and food move through a plant
  • Plants without flowers
