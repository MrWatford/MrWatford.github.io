🍃 Chapter 1 • Plentiful Plants

What we'll explore

Leaves that make food from sunlight, roots and stems, flowers, fruits, and seeds. Then trees, conifers, ferns, mosses, algae, fungi, and lichens, all showing the wonderful variety in God's creation.

Topics

  • How leaves make food
  • Roots and stems
  • Flowers, fruits, and seeds
  • Plant families to know
  • Broadleaf trees, conifers, and palms
  • Ferns, mosses, algae, and fungi

🐞 Chapter 2 • The World of Invertebrates

What we'll explore

How scientists classify living things, then animals without backbones: insects, spiders, strange creatures of the sea, and tiny protozoans you need a microscope to see.

Topics

  • How living things are classified
  • Insects and their main groups
  • Spiders and their relatives
  • Crustaceans, centipedes, and millipedes
  • Worms and mollusks
  • Strange sea creatures
  • Protozoans: tiny one-celled creatures

🌋 Chapter 3 • Our Fascinating Earth

What we'll explore

The ground beneath our feet: mountains, earthquakes, volcanoes, and the forces that shape the land God made.

Topics

  • The layers of the Earth
  • Earthquakes
  • Volcanoes and their types
  • Igneous, sedimentary, and metamorphic rocks
  • Gems, metals, and fuels
  • Caves
  • Earth as a giant magnet

🌌 Chapter 4 • God's Great Universe

What we'll explore

From our own planet out to the farthest galaxies: stars, constellations, the Moon, and the smaller objects that travel through space, all showing the greatness of the God who made them.

Topics

  • What space is like
  • Galaxies
  • Constellations
  • Stars
  • Early astronomers and their ideas
  • Our solar system, Earth, and Moon
  • Comets, asteroids, and meteors

🚀 Chapter 5 • Exploring Space

What we'll explore

How light lets us see across space, the telescopes that gather it, and how rockets work. Then the story of space travel, from the race to the Moon to space stations and robot probes visiting every planet.

Topics

  • Light and how it travels
  • Telescopes and other tools
  • How rockets fly
  • The race to the Moon
  • Space stations and the space shuttle
  • Satellites and space probes
  • Exploring the planets
  • How the universe began

  • What matter is
  • Atoms
  • The elements
  • Families of elements
  • Molecules and compounds
