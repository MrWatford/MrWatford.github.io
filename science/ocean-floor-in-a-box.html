Ocean Floor in a Box!

Drain away the water and build the hidden world God made beneath the waves!

📏 The rules

  • All 8 features. Build and label every required feature below.
  • Shoebox size. Build it inside a shoebox, or on cardboard about that size.
  • Shore to deep sea. Start with land on one end. Get deeper as you go across.

🗺️ Required features (label every one!)

  1. Continental Shelf: the shallow, gently sloping sea floor right next to the shore.
  2. Continental Slope: the steep drop-off at the edge of the shelf (the shelf break).
  3. Continental Rise: a gentle pile of sediment at the bottom of the slope.
  4. Submarine Canyon: a deep, narrow valley cut into the continental slope.
  1. Abyssal Plain: the wide, flat, deep ocean floor past the rise.
  2. Seamount: an underwater volcanic mountain with a pointed top.
  3. Guyot: a seamount with a flat top. (Say it "GEE-oh.")
  4. Trench or Ridge: pick one. A deep, narrow trench, OR a mid-ocean ridge with a rift down the middle.

⭐ Bonus points: add an atoll, a hydrothermal vent, sea creatures, or a tiny submersible!

🛠️ How to build it

  1. ✏️ Plan it. Sketch your floor on paper first, in order from shore to deep sea.
  2. ⛰️ Shape it. Use clay, play dough, or salt dough to build the floor inside the box.
  3. 🎨 Paint it. Paint the box walls blue for water. Use browns and grays for the floor.
  4. 🚩 Label it. Make toothpick flags with the name of each feature. Spell them right!
  5. 📖 Faith card. On an index card, write 1 to 2 sentences on how the ocean floor shows God's design. Add a verse.

💡 Salt dough: 2 cups flour + 1 cup salt + about ¾ cup water.
💡 Keep your shoebox LID! We need it for Sonar Day.

📡 Sonar Day (in class)

We will tape a lid with holes over a classmate's box. Then we will poke a marked skewer through each hole, record the depths, and graph the hidden ocean floor, just like sonar and echo sounding! Lift the lid to see how close your graph came.

"The sea is his, and he made it." Psalm 95:5
