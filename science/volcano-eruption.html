Volcano Eruption Demo

💨 Stage 1 • Smoke Rises

What we use

Dry ice dropped into warm water inside the volcano.

The science

Dry ice is frozen carbon dioxide (about −109°F)! It turns straight into a gas and makes a thick fog.

Real volcanoes: Before an eruption, volcanoes often puff out clouds of steam and gas.

✨ Stage 2 • Sparks Fly

What we use

Sparklers set in the crater of the volcano.

The science

Tiny bits of metal powder burn very hot and shoot out as glowing sparks.

Real volcanoes: Some volcanoes blast fountains of glowing lava and hot rock into the sky.

🔥 Stage 3 • Lava Flows

What we use

Baking soda, vinegar, and red and orange food coloring.

The science

Vinegar (an acid) meets baking soda (a base) and makes carbon dioxide gas. The bubbles push the foamy lava up and over!

Real volcanoes: Gas bubbles in magma help push it up and out of a real volcano.

⚠️ Be a safe scientist

  • Stay in your seat. Watch from a safe distance the whole time.
  • Hands off. Only Mr. Watford touches the dry ice and sparklers.
  • Think like a scientist. Watch closely and predict what happens next!
  • Demo only: never try the dry ice or sparkler stages at home.

🛠️ Your turn to build!

Now build your own volcano at home. On the due date, bring it to class and we'll make it erupt together!

  • Project dueWednesday, Nov. 4
  • SizeAt least 8 inches tall

The rules

📏 At least 8 inches tall

Measure from the bottom of your volcano to the top.

🏗️ Just build the volcano

You only make the structure. Eruption supplies will be provided in class!

How to make your volcano

  1. Bottle in the middle. Stand a plastic bottle in the center. This is where the eruption will happen!
  2. Add cardboard. Surround the bottle with cardboard to make the mountain shape.
  3. Paper strips. Dip strips of paper in paste (glue + water, or flour + water) and lay them over the cardboard.
  4. Paint & color. Paint or color your volcano to make it look realistic!

💡 Tips

Keep the bottle opening uncovered! Let the paper dry before you paint.
