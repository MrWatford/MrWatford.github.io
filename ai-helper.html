💬 AI Study Helper

Mr. Watford AI

An AI helper that explains problems, helps you practice, and finds things on this website. It's a computer program named after Mr. Watford, not Mr. Watford himself.

✏️ What it can help with

  • Pre-Algebra: step-by-step help, checking your work, and new practice problems.
  • Science: vocabulary, the scientific method, reading graphs, and planning projects.
  • Typing: home-row tips and practice paragraphs at your level.
  • Study tools: quizzes, flashcards, study plans, and project checklists.
  • Finding pages on this website, like study guides and project directions.

🛡️ Using it wisely

  • AI can make mistakes. Check important answers with Mr. Watford.
  • It helps you learn. It won't do tests or graded work for you.
  • Don't type your full name, address, phone number, or passwords.
  • No account needed. Your chat stays in this browser tab and disappears when you close it, or press Clear.
  • Your messages are sent to an AI service run by Cloudflare to get answers. Mr. Watford AI doesn't save them, and Cloudflare says it doesn't keep them or use them to train AI.
  • No one is watching this chat. If something is wrong or you feel unsafe, talk to a parent, teacher, or school counselor.
